About

A top-down survival game built for the CMNTY hackathon: fight waves out in the field, then port home to spend your coins on your base golem and grow your build wave after wave. Built with KAPLAY and Vite.

The full design document lives in the repo at docs/game-design-v2.md; the code is open under the repo at MickaelFleurus/CMNTY-Hackathon01. Sprites are from the Survivors Starter Kit by Sneznyj 3D (see docs/assets.md).
