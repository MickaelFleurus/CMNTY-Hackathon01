How a run works

  • Survive the wave: move, your pistol auto-fires, collect the XP gems and coins enemies drop.
  • When the wave ends, a portal appears if you're far from home - walk in, or just walk back to the golem yourself.
  • At the golem, the shop opens: one pick of the four upgrades (defense/offense × passive/active) per cycle.
  • The next wave is harder. Keep the golem alive - if the tower falls, the run is over.
  • Player death isn't the end: you respawn at the golem on a timer that grows with each death and shrinks when you clear a wave.
