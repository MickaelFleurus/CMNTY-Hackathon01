GOLEM NECROMANCER

A survivors × tower-defense hybrid — raise the golem, survive the horde

What is Golem Necromancer?

Top-down survival action where you defend a stone golem — your base — against endless waves of the dead.

Why it's different: it fuses Vampire Survivors-style auto-attacking hordes with tower-defense stakes — the base has HP, enemies lay siege to it, and when the golem falls, the run is over.

Every death costs more: one life heals the horde's memory — next death is run over.

The gameplay loop

FIGHT the wave→ PORTAL home→ SHOP at the golem→ WAKE the golem→ REPEAT, harder

Wave 1: the golem is an inert statue — you fight alone.

Wave end: the golem awakens — pick one of three styles, each a different way to play.

Breaks: spend coins in the golem's skill tree — buy as many nodes as your coins allow.

Golem styles: Guardian shields you, Berserker fights for you, Warden slows the horde.
